Walk / Neutral

Facing you is south. Walk left is east, walk right is west, walk back is reverse while still facing you.

Body Actions

Forward means anatomical forward. The 90° leg raise puts the femur horizontal and the knee at 90°, with the shin dropping downward. Only one leg pivots outward at a time. Leg Out and 90° Raise plant the stance foot first, then the free leg moves. Kneel left/right lower onto one knee; Kneel Both puts both knees on the ground. The ground-support pose keeps the feet planted while the torso folds and the hands reach the floor.

Head Controls

Nudges from the locked pose. The rest of the body stays put.

Locked Parts

After lock, move any part in either direction. The bot uses these same directions so one joint does not reset the others.

Keyboard

After locking: A/← walk left (east), D/→ walk right (west), S/↓ walk back (north), Space stop, N locked neutral. Facing you is south.

Walk Model

The gait keeps the knee in normal backward flexion only. During swing the knee folds first, then the femur advances, then the shin extends downward for heel strike. Lower-back motion is small and upper-back motion is slightly larger, while hip-out and arm-out controls remain available for poses like T-pose and ground support.
Tip: resize the browser freely. The 3D view stays visible while the controls reflow. Front / Side / Back now pan smoothly between views instead of snapping instantly. Double-click the viewer to ease back to the front view.
